## Approved design

Flow: **Challenge → continuous stretch gesture → applicable concern or Skip → build momentum → Seal pact → return review**. Doable goes directly from stretch to momentum with prediction skipped.

Keep exactly three meaningful, equally available actions: Start a real conversation (update plus open question), Make a clear request (specific helpful change plus reason), Share a different view (respectful difference plus reason). Reply or agreement is never required. Keep commitment criteria, Details and sequential review. No category selector, displayed difficulty numbers or automatic smaller/larger suggestions.

Keep a stable guardian stage above one changing interaction panel, approximately equal shares of available viewport height. All controls, instructions, errors and Details stay below the character. Only the lower panel scrolls on short screens or enlarged text. Controls remain at least 44 px tall with visible keyboard focus. Persist each answer before advancing or reacting.

### Continuous stretch and concern routing

Ask “How much of a stretch would this be?” with “Drag to show how hard it would be for you.” Use a free 0–100 slider with fine 0.1-point internal precision and no category-anchor snapping or visible score. Domain logic derives manageable/Doable for 0–33, stretch/A stretch above 33–66, and too_much/Too much above 66.

One HTML/CSS manga speech balloon sits beside the guardian’s face with its tail toward him. It starts at “Your call.” and shows the current preview band during pointer or keyboard adjustment; it reflects the player’s choice. Placement follows the character’s rendered size and accommodates enlarged text without covering his face or upper body. The former three label buttons are removed.

Dragging previews; release or a track tap commits once. Arrow keys adjust five points, Home/End reach endpoints, Enter/Space confirms, and Escape/cancellation restores the previous position. The untouched initial position never saves automatically. The balloon and slider preview remain separate from persisted state.

A Doable confirmation atomically saves numeric position, derived effort, skipped prediction and entry into sealing. The other bands retain the applicable concerns and Skip without escalating worry severity. Too much briefly surprises, then reassures, and retains Change action/Pause for now. Before the first saved tap, Back returns to stretch for Doable and to concerns for the other bands. Details temporarily replaces the active lower panel.

### Stronger momentum and explicit seal

Use the single heading “Tap to build momentum.”, replaced by “Ready to seal.” at full charge. Keep only useful pace and unsigned-readiness hints. A stationary 104 px target adds 5 points per completed activation; after 450 ms without a tap, active charge drains at 12 points/sec. Twenty steady taps fill it in about 5–7 seconds at three to four taps/sec with no minimum timer. At my pace uses the same gain without decay.

A native click counts mouse, touch or keyboard activation once; repeated Enter/Space keydowns cannot add progress. Keep the separate Seal pact button outside the tapping target. Full readiness latches, enables/focuses this button and remains unsigned until explicit activation. The first accepted tap permanently locks choices even if charge later drains to zero.

Taps and pause snapshots persist charge. Hide, blur and pagehide pause active time; reload restores the last saved charge without offline decay. Errors pause input and retain the current prompt, last saved answer and recoverable error. Failed seal writes never sign or celebrate.

### Reused artwork and readable comic timing

Reuse the accepted thirteen transparent body/seal assets without generating another raster image. Preserve the mature tanuki, fur markings, opaque white fur, proportions and red scarf. All poses and the answering mark preload/decode before expression switching. The first-alpha gate, authorized Python isnet-anime processing and light/dark/colored-background acceptance remain recorded in [artwork notes](../../art-direction.md). No atlas cropping, contrast filtering or multiply blending returns.

Saved-answer expressions hold for 1,200 ms unless a newer explicit answer replaces them. Taps retain separate 160 ms impulses. Principal charging poses hold at least 650 ms and keep only the newest pending pose, without a queue. After 1,200 ms without input, the guardian settles to relaxed attention.

A successfully saved seal triggers a 2,400 ms sequence: anticipation 600 ms → smear 180 ms → strike/ink impact 620 ms → delighted settle 1,000 ms. Shared constants in lib/guardian-motion.ts supply both CSS delays/durations and reaction cleanup. Effects remain inside the stage. Honest reports are pleased, encouraging or accepting. Reduced motion keeps static faces, balloon and comic symbols, including the delighted face and stamp; it removes shake, zoom, sweeping and bounce. Failed saves and reopened signed pacts never celebrate.

### State compatibility and review

Keep the existing v2 format, keys, catalogue identity and daily/demo separation. Add optional numeric anticipatedValue and derive its effort in domain logic. Old choosing drafts without a number reopen at the stretch step, using the category midpoint as an uncommitted preview while preserving the old saved answer. No historical numeric measurement is invented. Old catalogue refresh remains a guarded write.

Preserve existing sealing charge, pace, started status and readiness under the new tuning. Preserve legacy traces verbatim: incomplete traces migrate to an empty meter with their original action/catalogue/ink record; completed traces remain eligible for explicit sealing. Signed wording, dates and historical reviews remain unchanged. Taps never create handwriting data.

Review stays sequential: outcome → applicable comparison → effort, or outcome → non-attempt reason/Skip → summary → explicit Close pact. Automatically skipped predictions omit comparison; historical explicit predictions retain it. Back permits corrections, and reload derives the next unanswered prompt. WebMCP read_ritual includes numeric stretch when recorded plus the meter and legacy trace; no tool taps or seals on the player’s behalf.
